Privacy

No account. Everything stays on this device: progress, notes, your start point and target, and your recordings. Nothing is uploaded.

Location is asked for only when you tap My location or start a hunt. Discoveries keep only a position rounded to about 100 m.

Camera is used only while the SLS camera is open. Frames are analysed on the device and are never stored or uploaded unless you tap Snapshot.

Microphone is used only while you record or while Sensors are on (a volume reading, nothing is recorded). Recordings are saved in this browser and can be deleted any time.

Body tracking runs on your device. The first time, the tracker files (about 15 MB) are fetched from jsDelivr and Google storage, which see your IP address as any download does; the video never leaves the phone.

Maps load tiles from OpenStreetMap, which sees the tiles your phone asks for. Visited tiles are cached for offline use.

Streaming? Keep your live position off screen.
